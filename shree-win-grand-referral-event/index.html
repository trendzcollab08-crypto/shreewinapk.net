ShreeWin Grand Referral Event 2026: Milestones, Rewards and Rules

The ShreeWin Grand Referral Event 2026 rewards eligible agents who build a verified direct referral network. Your leaderboard position depends on the number of active invited members recorded during the event. This page explains every milestone, qualification rule, reward option and claim step.

02

Who counts as an active invited member?

For this event, an active invited member is a unique direct subordinate who registers through your ShreeWin invitation link. The member must make at least one deposit of ₹500 or more and complete eligible activity on WinGo or TRX lottery games.

A new registration alone is not an active invite. The deposit and eligible game activity must be recorded against the same direct subordinate during the event. Repeated accounts, incomplete registrations and members outside your direct referral line do not meet this definition.

  • Unique direct subordinate
  • Registration tracked through your invitation link
  • At least one deposit of ₹500 or more
  • Eligible WinGo or TRX lottery activity
  • Activity recorded during the event period
03

How referral tracking works

Open your ShreeWin Agency page and copy your own invitation link. When a new member registers through that link, the invitation code connects the account with your direct team. Ask the member to check that the code is visible before submitting registration.

The event counter updates only after the invited member completes all active-player conditions. Use the direct team list and Promotion Center counter together. Do not count a registration manually until it appears as eligible in the event record.

04

How leaderboard qualification works

Milestones set the minimum active-invite count for each leaderboard group. Final rank depends on the verified event result, so several agents can reach the same threshold without receiving the same position.

Check your recorded count, current position and event closing rules inside Promotion Center. Product availability and cash alternatives should be read from the reward screen connected with your final position.

05

How to check progress and claim a reward

Login to ShreeWin, open Promotion Center and choose the Grand Referral Event. The event page shows your active-invite progress and reward status. When a qualified milestone becomes available, open it and tap Receive.

After claiming, open Wallet and check the credit record. Save a screenshot of the unlocked milestone, claim confirmation and Wallet reference. These details help Customer Support check the reward if the balance does not update.

  • Login and open Promotion Center
  • Choose the Grand Referral Event
  • Check the active-invite count and rank
  • Open the unlocked reward
  • Tap Receive
  • Check Wallet and save the claim record
06

1.0x wagering and withdrawal condition

The event terms list a 1.0x wagering requirement for milestone rewards. This means the credited reward must complete the valid activity required by the event before it becomes eligible for withdrawal.

After the condition is complete, choose an available withdrawal method and check the receiving details. Account verification, correct bank information and payment-network processing can still affect a withdrawal request. Read the event record and Withdrawal screen before confirming.

07

Grand Referral Event and the six-level Agency system

This milestone event is separate from the normal six-level Agency structure. The event rewards final direct active-invite milestones, while daily turnover commission follows eligible team activity and the rates shown in Agency.

Any fixed salary or daily commission has its own qualifying rules. Do not add those values to the one-time milestone reward unless both records have been approved separately. Use Agency for team commission and Promotion Center for the Grand Referral Event.

08

Why an active invite may not appear

First check whether the member registered through your exact invitation link. Then check that the account is a unique direct subordinate, the qualifying deposit is at least ₹500 and the required WinGo or TRX lottery activity is complete.

The counter may remain unchanged while an eligible transaction is still pending. If every condition is complete but the invite is missing, contact Customer Support with your agent ID, the event name, date and clear screenshots. Never send another person’s password or OTP.

  • Invitation code was not linked
  • Deposit is below ₹500
  • Deposit is still pending
  • Eligible game activity is incomplete
  • Member is not a direct subordinate
  • Event record is still updating
09

Records every ShreeWin agent should keep

Keep your own agent records organised. Save the invitation link, direct active-invite count, milestone status, claim date and Wallet credit reference. A dated screenshot is useful when the displayed count changes.

Do not operate another person’s account and never ask for a password, OTP, UPI PIN or withdrawal password. The invited member should complete registration, deposits and game choices from their own account.

  • Agent ID and invitation link
  • Direct team record
  • Active-invite count
  • Milestone and rank status
  • Claim confirmation
  • Wallet credit reference
10

Checks to complete before joining the event

Open the live event page and read the start and closing dates, eligible games, active-player definition, product or cash option, claim window and wagering condition. Use the information shown for your account because a promotion can be changed or closed after its event period.

Referral rewards are based on verified qualification. Explain the active-player conditions clearly when sharing your link, and do not promise a rank, product, cash reward or regular income before the platform confirms the result.

ShreeWin Grand Referral Event FAQs

What is the ShreeWin Grand Referral Event 2026?+

It is a referral milestone tournament for eligible ShreeWin agents. The event counts verified direct active invites and places qualifying agents into leaderboard groups. Each group has a listed product reward or cash option. Progress and reward status appear inside Promotion Center.

What is an active invite in this ShreeWin event?+

An active invite is a unique direct subordinate who registers through your invitation link, completes at least one deposit of ₹500 or more and takes part in eligible WinGo or TRX lottery activity during the event. Registration alone does not complete the active-player requirement.

What are the ShreeWin referral milestone levels?+

The listed active-invite milestones are 50+, 200+, 500+, 1,200+, 2,500+ and 5,000+. These numbers connect with leaderboard groups from 51st–200th place up to the Champion position. Final rank still depends on the verified event result.

How do I check my active-invite count?+

Login to ShreeWin and open Promotion Center. Select the Grand Referral Event to see the count recorded for your account. You can also compare it with your direct team list in Agency. A registration may not count until every active-player condition is complete.

How do I claim a Grand Referral Event reward?+

Open the event in Promotion Center after the milestone shows as unlocked. Tap Receive and then check the credit record in Wallet. Save the claim confirmation and Wallet reference. If the credit does not appear, contact Customer Support with your agent ID and screenshots.

What does the 1.0x wagering requirement mean?+

The event terms require valid activity equal to the credited milestone reward before eligible withdrawal. Check the progress shown with the reward and complete that requirement first. Account verification and correct withdrawal details may also be required when you submit the request.

Is the referral reward guaranteed after registration?+

No. A registration must become a verified direct active invite, your account must reach a listed milestone and the platform must confirm the final leaderboard position. Product or cash availability, claim status and other conditions are shown in the live event record.

Where can I get help for a missing referral or reward?+

Open Customer Support from ShreeWin Settings. Send your agent ID, event name, milestone, date, active-invite count and clear screenshots. For a missing Wallet credit, include the claim reference. Never send your password, OTP, UPI PIN or withdrawal password.
