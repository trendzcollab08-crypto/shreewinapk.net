Contact and support help

Contact ShreeWinAPK.net

Use this page to report a broken link, content problem or accessibility issue. For a ShreeWin account or payment problem, open the customer-support button in Settings.

01

Website issues you can report

You can report a broken page, wrong link, spelling mistake, outdated screenshot, accessibility issue or copyright concern. Include the page URL and explain what needs to be fixed.

A public email address and contact form are not available on this website yet.

  • Broken page or internal link
  • Spelling or content correction
  • Outdated information
  • Accessibility problem
  • Copyright or brand concern
02

ShreeWin account and payment problems

ShreeWinAPK.net cannot reset your password, unlock your account, trace a deposit or approve a withdrawal. Open Customer Support in ShreeWin Settings for these problems.

Share your user ID, issue type, date, amount and transaction reference when needed. Hide private details in screenshots before you send them.

03

Information you must never share

Never send a password, OTP, withdrawal password, UPI PIN, bank PIN, card PIN, complete card number, account recovery code or remote-access code. Support should not need these details to read a complaint.

Do not send an identity document to an email address or chat account that you have not verified. Do not pay a person who asks for a fee to unlock support or release a payment.

04

If your complaint is not solved

Keep the complaint number, screenshots and transaction record. Reply through the same support option and ask for the matter to be reviewed again. Explain the problem and the help you need in one clear message.

For a bank, UPI or crypto-wallet issue, also use the complaint process provided by that payment service.
