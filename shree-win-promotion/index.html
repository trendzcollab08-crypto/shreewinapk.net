Promotion and activity help

ShreeWin Promotion and Activity Guide

The ShreeWin app has separate Activity and Promotion areas. Open the current offer and read its complete rules before you deposit or claim a reward.

01

Where to find promotions and activities

Use the bottom menu to open Activity or Promotion. Activity shows tasks and reward options. Promotion shows the offers available for your account.

Open an offer to check its date, account requirement, required action and claim button.

02

First-deposit offer

The First Deposit Bonus page shows four one-time tiers: deposit ₹300 to receive ₹48, ₹500 to receive ₹108, ₹1,000 to receive ₹188, or ₹5,000 to receive ₹488. Each account can use this activity once.

Open the offer in your account before payment and read every condition shown there. Bonus amounts follow the promotion rules.

  • Deposit ₹300 — bonus ₹48
  • Deposit ₹500 — bonus ₹108
  • Deposit ₹1,000 — bonus ₹188
  • Deposit ₹5,000 — bonus ₹488
03

Check the turnover rules

Read who can join, the campaign dates, required deposit, reward amount, claim button and account limits. Turnover is the valid game activity needed before some bonus amounts can be withdrawn.

The bonus has a one-times valid-bet or coding-turnover rule. Open the offer details to see which games count and how the requirement is calculated.

04

How to claim a promotion

Open the offer from Activity or Promotion and follow its steps. Complete the required action and tap Claim if that button is available. Save a screenshot of the offer rules and completed status.

For a deposit offer, use only the payment details shown after you tap Deposit in ShreeWin.

05

Promotion not received

Check whether the offer is active, whether your account meets its rules and whether the required transaction is complete. Also check if you need to tap Claim instead of waiting for the reward to be added automatically.

If the issue remains, open customer support in Settings. Share the offer name, date, amount, transaction reference and screenshots. Never share your password, OTP, UPI PIN or withdrawal password.
