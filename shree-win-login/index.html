ShreeWin Login Guide

To complete the ShreeWin login, open the login page and choose Phone Number or Email. Enter the details linked to your account, check them once and then tap Login.

Reviewed by ShreeWinAPK.net Editorial TeamUpdated 4 September 2026

01

Details needed for ShreeWin login

You can log in with your registered phone number or email address. For phone login, select the correct country code and enter your mobile number and password. For email login, enter the email address linked to your account and your password.

Use the same method you selected during registration. ShreeWinAPK.net does not ask you to enter your login details on this page.

02

ShreeWin login steps

Tap Login on this website. The ShreeWin login page has Phone Number and Email tabs. Choose the method linked to your account, enter your details, check for typing mistakes and tap Login.

If you use a shared phone, do not select Remember Password. Log out after use and remove any saved password from the browser.

  • Open the login page
  • Choose Phone Number or Email
  • Enter your registered details
  • Check the country code and spelling
  • Complete the OTP step if asked
  • Tap Login
03

Forgotten password and OTP problems

Tap Forgot Password and choose phone reset. Enter your registered mobile number, create a new password, confirm it and enter the verification code sent to you. Type the code only on the ShreeWin reset page.

If the OTP does not arrive, check the mobile number and country code, wait for the resend button and make sure your phone can receive SMS. Do not request many codes at the same time.

04

Incorrect password or locked account

Check for capital letters, extra spaces or an old saved password. Use Forgot Password instead of trying many passwords again and again.

If recovery does not work, open the customer-support button in Settings. Share your user ID and the error message, but never share your password, OTP, UPI PIN or withdrawal password.

05

Login page opens but the account does not load

If the Login button works but the next screen stays blank, first change between mobile data and Wi-Fi and reload the page once. Close old ShreeWin tabs so that you do not continue through an expired session.

If the problem continues, note the time, browser name and exact message on the screen. Customer Support can use these details to separate a password problem from a page-loading or account-status problem.

06

Keep your account access private

Use a different password from your email, bank and UPI apps. Do not give remote screen access to a person claiming they can fix your login. Check the web address before you enter any credential.

For account unlock or recovery help, open Customer Support in ShreeWin Settings and share the error message without sharing your password or OTP.
