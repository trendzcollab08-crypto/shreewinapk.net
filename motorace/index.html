ShreeWin MotoRace Guide

Find MotoRace in the ShreeWin Lottery section. Open the game to check the active period, countdown, available choices and result history.

01

How to open MotoRace

Open the ShreeWin homepage, tap Lottery and look for the MotoRace card near TRX WinGo. Tap the card to open the game page.

If the card position has changed, check the full Lottery list in the app.

02

Read the active round details

Check the selected round or period and the countdown shown on the game screen. Review the available choices and understand the confirmation step before submitting an entry.

Tap Help or Rules to see the MotoRace periods, choice types, entry limits and closing rules.

03

Check the result and history

When a round is complete, match its period number with the result shown in Game History. Also check your entry or transaction record so you are looking at the correct round.

If a record remains pending, refresh the page and wait for an update. Save the round number, date, time and screenshot before using the support button in Settings.

04

Account and play safety

Keep your password, OTP, UPI PIN and withdrawal password private. Customer Support can check a round using its reference number without these secret details.

Choose your time and amount limits before joining a paid round. You must be 18+ and follow the gaming rules in your area.
